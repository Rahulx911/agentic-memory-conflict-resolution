Architecture

Two-tier long-term memory, wired through a LangGraph state machine.

Structured memory · Postgres

Entities (equipment, zones) and an append-only fact ledger — status, last-serviced, confidence, timestamp — with a full write/overwrite audit trail for provenance.

Episodic memory · Qdrant

Per-session conversation summaries, embedded for semantic recall — "has anything like this happened before" across sessions, not just exact fact lookups.

The conflict policy is legible by design

Simple and auditable rather than learned — anyone can read classify_stakes() and know exactly why a contradiction was or wasn't auto-resolved.

Low stakes

Auto-resolved by observed_at recency, not insertion order — a backdated correction can't clobber a fact observed more recently. Exact ties fall back to a human, since recency alone can't decide.

High stakes

Safety-relevant attributes (equipment/zone status) and low-confidence observations never auto-resolve. Staged as PENDING_CONFIRMATION and logged as an open escalation for a human to accept or reject.
